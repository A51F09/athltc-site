ATHLTC

Fitness, fasting and recovery

Train, fast and recover in one app.

ATHLTC logs your lifts, meals, sleep and steps. It also has a full Ramadan mode with suhoor and iftar times, a live countdown and a daily fast log.

The screens on this page come from a demo account with sample data.

Live

Ramadan mode

Turn it on from the Summary and ATHLTC works out the month, the times and a training window around your fast.

  • Dates that follow the calendarThe month starts from the Islamic calendar automatically. If your community sights the moon on another day, set the first day yourself.
  • Suhoor and iftar from your locationTimes are calculated on your device. Choose from 12 methods and add minute adjustments to match your mosque's timetable.
  • A countdown that stays liveSee how far through today's fast you are and when iftar arrives, with a suggested training window afterwards.
  • A log for every dayMark each day fasted, excused or missed. Meals in your food log switch to Suhoor and Iftar.

Live

Training

Log sets with a tap, start a day from your plan, and watch your consistency build.

  • Start from a planPick a day from a saved program and the exercises, sets and weights are ready. Missing weights come from your last session.
  • Import plans from textPaste a plan, type one, or upload a text or CSV file. You see exactly how it was read before you save it.
  • Progress you can seeA calendar of training days, your streak, your weekly count and personal records for your main lifts.

Live

Food, sleep and steps

Set daily goals and watch them fill through the day.

  • Calories and macrosLog meals with calories and optional protein, carbs and fat, against a daily goal.
  • Sleep with a goalLog bedtime and wake time, rate the night, and compare a week of sleep to your target.
  • Apple Health importChoose your Apple Health export to bring in steps, sleep and calories. The file is read on your device and only daily totals are saved.

Your look

Black by default, with light and automatic themes and seven accent colors.

  • Seven accentsBlue, green, orange, red, purple, pink or teal, applied across the whole app.
